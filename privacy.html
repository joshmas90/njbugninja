Privacy.

This site is deliberately simple about data. Here is exactly what it does and does not collect.

What this site collects

Nothing is submitted to a server through this website. The quote form and ZIP check run entirely in your browser: pressing the button opens your own messaging app with a pre-written text to 609-313-6317. Whether you send it is up to you.

Text messages and calls

If you text or call, your name, phone number and whatever you tell us about the property are kept only to quote, schedule and perform the service. They are not sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log the request in accordance with Google's privacy policy.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Hostinger, which keeps standard server access logs (IP address, time, page requested) for security and operations.
