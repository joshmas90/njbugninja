PRIVACY.
SIMPLE AND CLEAR.

The Mosquito Ninja app can use your current location when you choose to check the service area. Mosquito Ninja does not receive or store your precise coordinates.

App & website privacy

  • Saved app appointments remain on the customer device
  • Appointment reminders are scheduled locally through iOS
  • Location is requested only when the customer chooses to check the service area
  • iOS location and geocoding services identify the county and ZIP code for the check
  • Mosquito Ninja does not receive or store the customer's precise coordinates
  • The app downloads public county and ZIP coverage rules and compares the result on the device
  • The website quote form submits the contact and property details you enter to Mosquito Ninja’s company email inbox at service@njbugninja.com
  • The website does not create a separate lead database from quote-form submissions
  • The website county and optional ZIP check runs in your browser against public coverage rules; it does not request precise location
  • Website preparation checkmarks are temporary and are not submitted to Mosquito Ninja
  • Hosting providers may maintain standard server logs
  • No intentional third-party marketing pixel is included in this build
  • Website interaction hooks are local unless an analytics service is separately configured
  • If data practices change, this page should be updated first
