Before the first visit

Do you treat both mosquitoes and ticks?
Yes. They live in different parts of the property and are treated differently, so most yards benefit from addressing both.
How do I get a quote?
Use the quote form on the home page or text 609-313-6317 with your town and a few words about the property. The form prepares a text message on your own device; nothing is sent to a server.
Do you come out to look at the property first?
The treatment plan follows the layout of the yard, so the property is looked at before anything is applied. For most homes that happens on the first visit.
Is my town covered?
Coverage is confirmed by ZIP code rather than listed on the site. Send yours and Josh will tell you directly.

During and after treatment

When can we use the yard again?
It depends on the product used that day. You will receive the product-specific re-entry instructions in writing after each visit. Follow those rather than a general rule.
What about pets, children and the vegetable garden?
Tell Josh about them before the visit. Where and how product is applied takes them into account, and the written instructions for that visit cover any areas to keep clear.
Will this get rid of every mosquito?
No, and be wary of anyone who says otherwise. Weather, standing water on neighboring properties and habitat all affect pressure. Good treatment makes a noticeable difference; it does not create a barrier.
How often is treatment needed?
That depends on the property, the season and the pests involved. Josh will recommend a schedule after seeing the yard rather than selling a package sight unseen.

Practical questions

Do you do one-time treatments before a party or event?
Ask. Timing relative to the event and the re-entry window for the product both matter, so it is best to text a week or more ahead.
Do you treat commercial properties?
Yes: outdoor dining, event lawns, pool decks, courtyards and grounds. Visits are scheduled around your hours.
Who does the work?
Josh. The person who quotes the job is the person who does it and the person who answers the phone afterward.
