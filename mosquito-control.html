Mosquito control that starts where they rest.

Adult mosquitoes spend the day in cool, shaded, humid places, not out on the open lawn. Treatment aimed at those places is what makes an evening outside possible again.

What the service is built around

Mosquito pressure on a property comes from a few predictable places: dense shrubs and hedges, the undersides of leaves, ivy and groundcover, shaded fence lines, and anywhere water sits for more than a few days. Weather and neighboring lots matter too, which is why no honest company promises total elimination.

Walking the property first

Before anything is applied, Josh looks at the layout: where the shade falls in the afternoon, where the foliage is thickest, where the gutters and low spots collect water, and where you actually sit. The treatment plan follows that, not a standard route.

Application focused on harborage

Product is directed at resting and harborage areas rather than sprayed evenly over everything green. It is more effective, uses less product, and keeps the open areas of the yard as they should be.

Standing water you cannot remove

Some water sources are permanent: a drainage swale, a low corner, a decorative pond. Those can be addressed as part of the plan, and the ones you can empty yourself get pointed out.

After the visit

You receive the product-specific re-entry instructions for that treatment in writing. Those instructions, not a rule of thumb, tell you when the yard is yours again.

No force-field promises.

Outdoor conditions change and pressure can return, especially after heavy rain. Good treatment makes a real, noticeable difference. It does not repeal biology.
