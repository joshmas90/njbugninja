PROFESSIONAL PRODUCTS

THE RIGHT TOOL.
FOR THE RIGHT TARGET.

We do not treat every property with one generic recipe. Product choice is based on the target pest, habitat, weather, site conditions and the current product label.

Core liquid treatment

ONEGUARD MULTI MoA

A multi-action concentrate we can select for mosquito-focused outdoor treatment. Its formulation combines adult control, quick knockdown, a synergist and an insect growth regulator in one product.

Residual liquid treatment

DEMAND CS

A microencapsulated lambda-cyhalothrin residual option for labeled mosquito and tick treatment sites, including foliage, shrubs, perimeter areas and transition zones.

Dry / standing-water control

BTI LARVICIDES

Biological granules or briquets can be used in label-permitted standing water that cannot simply be eliminated, targeting mosquito larvae before they emerge as adults.

Extended larval control

IGR LARVICIDE GRANULES

Methoprene-based granular larvicides are another professional tool that may be selected for suitable standing-water habitats when longer-duration larval control is appropriate.

Not every product is used on every visit. Product selection, application site, rate, PPE and re-entry instructions follow the current product label and applicable requirements. Exact treatment details are confirmed for the individual property.
