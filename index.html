All questions
Do you treat both mosquitoes and ticks?
Yes. They are treated differently because they live in different parts of the property, and most yards benefit from addressing both.
When can we use the yard again?
It depends on the product used that day. You will get the specific re-entry instructions for that product in writing after every visit. Follow those rather than a rule of thumb.
Will this get rid of every mosquito?
No, and be wary of anyone who says otherwise. Weather, standing water on neighboring properties, and habitat all affect pressure. Good treatment makes a real, noticeable difference; it does not create a force field.
