TICK CONTROL.
FOR SOUTH JERSEY PROPERTIES.

Tick control for South Jersey homes and outdoor spaces, with attention to the transition between the lawn, woods and brush. Tell us where you have noticed activity and how people and pets use the property.

Focus on the property edges

Tick service starts with a look at the landscape and the places you move through every day. A path beside the woods, a shaded boundary or brush near an outdoor seating area can call for a different discussion from an open lawn.

Areas considered for tick control

  • Woodline and lawn transitions
  • Leaf litter and low brush
  • Stone walls and shaded perimeter habitat
  • Paths and outdoor spaces used by people and pets

Wooded properties and seasonal activity

Tick activity and treatment needs can change as property conditions and weather change. A wooded edge, shaded transition or leaf-litter area may remain relevant even when the open lawn is not the main concern. The service schedule is discussed for the actual property and current activity rather than based on a single calendar interval.

Property conditions that can support tick habitat

Share the locations where you have noticed ticks and any wooded, brushy or shaded sections near the areas you use. Leaf litter, low brush, stone walls and transition zones are all useful details when describing the property before treatment is discussed.

People, pets and access after service

There is no blanket waiting time that applies to every application. Follow the product-specific re-entry instructions provided for the actual treatment before people or pets return to treated areas. If children, pets, edible gardens or other sensitive use areas affect how you use the property, point them out before service so they can be considered in the visit plan.

Weather and service timing

Weather can affect outdoor application decisions and scheduling. If conditions are not suitable for the planned treatment, timing may need to change. Visit-specific guidance is provided for the actual application.

What happens before and after service

You deal directly with one point of contact for the quote and the work. The property layout and the pest concern guide the service discussion, and product-specific re-entry instructions are provided in writing for the visit.

Also dealing with mosquitoes?

Mention both concerns in your quote request. South Jersey mosquito control looks at shaded vegetation, damp areas and other mosquito habitat, while tick service pays particular attention to edges and ground-level cover.

Outdoor pest activity varies with weather, habitat and neighboring properties. Service does not come with a promise of zero ticks or mosquitoes.

Send your town or ZIP code, the areas of concern and a short description of the property. See the South Jersey mosquito and tick control service area or the tick control FAQs. Current coverage and scheduling are confirmed directly.
